# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 August 2026

Goodgorithm is an ad-free, account-free feed of positive public posts from [Bluesky](https://bsky.app) and [Mastodon](https://joinmastodon.org). This policy describes what the app and website do with data. The short version: we don't collect anything about you, and there's no account to attach anything to.

## No account, no profile, no tracking

- There is no sign-up or login. We never ask for your name, email, phone number, or any other identifying detail.
- There is no personalization. Everyone opening the same feed at the same moment sees the same ranked posts — the ranking depends only on each post's own content, never on you.
- There are no advertising, analytics, or tracking tools of any kind — no third-party SDKs, no cookies set by us, no fingerprinting.

## What stays on your device

The app remembers a few conveniences locally, in your browser or the app's own storage: roughly where you were in the feed, and small display preferences. This never leaves your device, is never sent to us, and you can clear it any time by clearing the app's or browser's site data.

## What happens over the network

- **The feed:** the app requests the ranked list of posts from Goodgorithm's own API. That request carries only what any web request carries — your device's IP address and a user-agent string — and is not linked to any identity.
- **Post media:** images and video in a post are loaded directly from Bluesky's and Mastodon's servers, not proxied through us. Those servers receive your IP address and user-agent as part of serving the file, the same as opening the post on Bluesky or Mastodon would. Their handling of that is covered by their own privacy policies.
- **Infrastructure:** the website is served through [Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/) and the API runs on [Railway](https://railway.com/legal/privacy). As with any hosted service, these providers process requests on our behalf and may keep short-lived operational logs (e.g. for abuse prevention and debugging). We do not use them to build any profile of you.

## The posts themselves

Every post shown is already public — published openly on Bluesky or Mastodon by its author. Goodgorithm only reads the same public feeds anyone can, ranks them, and displays them with a link back to the original. If you're an author and want a post reviewed or removed from the feed, contact us at the address below.

## Children

Goodgorithm is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the text and the date above. Because there's no account, we have no way to notify you directly — the current version always lives at this page.
